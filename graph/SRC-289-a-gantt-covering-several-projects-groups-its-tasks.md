# SRC-289: A Gantt covering several projects groups its tasks by project

A Gantt chart covering several projects, such as a saved Gantt view, groups its tasks by project: one heading row per project, with that project's tasks below it.

As received, in the design session of 2026-11-23, recorded in issue #256 (the UI/UX review of the demo deployment). Asked, about item 35 ("A Gantt view covering several projects shows no project"), "How should it show the project?", with the options "Group by project" ("One heading row per project, its tasks below it."), "Project column" and "Both", the owner chose "Group by project".

Not stated: the order of the projects.
