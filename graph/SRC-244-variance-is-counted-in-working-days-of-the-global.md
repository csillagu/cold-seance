# SRC-244: Variance is counted in working days of the global calendar

Variance is counted in working days of the global calendar: its weekend days and company holidays are not counted.

As received, in the design session of 2026-11-06, in the work on the variance calculator (SC-044, issue #119, PR #164). Claude reported that TC-092 says it is not stated whether variance is counted in calendar days or working days, and offered three options: calendar days; working days on the global calendar, skipping the weekend and company holidays; or working days on each assignee's personal working calendar. The example given was a task planned to start on Friday that starts on Monday: +3 in calendar days, +1 in working days. The owner answered: "Option 2."
