# DD-013: Shared people can be made eligible or assigned only with read access to the project

## Decision

Any project can make anyone from the pool eligible or assign them, as long as that person has at least read access to the project.

## Alternatives considered

Any project with read-write access can assign anyone from the pool, versus requiring the person's or their lead's agreement.

## Trade-offs

Gives up requiring the person's or their lead's agreement.

## Serves

This decision serves [SRC-046](SRC-046-people-live-in-one-global-pool.md).
