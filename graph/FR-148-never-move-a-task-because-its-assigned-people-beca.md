# FR-148: Never move a task because its assigned people became unable to do it

When a change made after a task is planned, such as someone adding a day off or someone being assigned, leaves its assigned people unable to do it in its time, the system must not move the task ([SRC-127](SRC-127-a-change-in-availability-turns-a-task-red-without-m.md)).

It serves [UC-003](UC-003-assign-people-to-a-task.md), [UC-020](UC-020-maintain-my-own-working-calendar.md), [UC-026](UC-026-record-my-absence.md) and [UC-028](UC-028-maintain-the-company-holiday-calendar.md).
