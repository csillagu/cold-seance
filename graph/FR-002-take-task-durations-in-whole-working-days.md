# FR-002: Take task durations in whole working days

The system must take a task's duration in whole working days ([DD-006](DD-006-durations-are-whole-working-days.md)), and derive its planned finish from its planned start and that duration over the task's working days ([SRC-023](SRC-023-minimum-task-fields.md), [SRC-121](SRC-121-scheduling-skips-non-working-days.md)).

It serves [UC-001](UC-001-plan-a-task-in-a-project.md) and [UC-002](UC-002-reschedule-work-in-a-project.md).
