# FR-020: Turn a task red when its assigned people cannot do it in its time

When the people assigned to a task cannot do it in the task's time, the system must show the task in red ([SRC-122](SRC-122-a-task-turns-red-when-its-assigned-people-cannot-d.md)). A later change that causes this, such as someone adding a day off or someone being assigned, must not move the task ([SRC-127](SRC-127-a-change-in-availability-turns-a-task-red-without-m.md)).

Not stated: what, besides a day off falling inside the task, counts as the assigned people being unable to do the task in its time.

It serves [UC-003](UC-003-assign-people-to-a-task.md), [UC-020](UC-020-maintain-my-own-working-calendar.md), [UC-026](UC-026-record-my-absence.md) and [UC-028](UC-028-maintain-the-company-holiday-calendar.md).
