# FR-020: Turn a task red when its assigned people cannot do it in its time

When one of a task's assigned people is overloaded ([FR-042](FR-042-mark-a-person-overloaded-on-any-day-their-load-exc.md)), or on holiday, on a day within the task, the system must show the task in red ([SRC-122](SRC-122-a-task-turns-red-when-its-assigned-people-cannot-d.md), [SRC-170](SRC-170-a-task-turns-red-when-an-assignee-is-overloaded-or.md)).

It serves [UC-003](UC-003-assign-people-to-a-task.md), [UC-020](UC-020-maintain-my-own-working-calendar.md), [UC-026](UC-026-record-my-absence.md) and [UC-028](UC-028-maintain-the-company-holiday-calendar.md).
