# SRC-153: People subscribe to issues and projects; write and admin roles are subscribed automatically

People can subscribe to issues and to projects, and get the notifications ([SRC-015](SRC-015-notifications-for-new-red-flags-proposals-waiting.md)) about what they subscribed to. People with the write or admin role in a project are subscribed to it automatically ([SRC-092](SRC-092-read-write-role.md), [SRC-093](SRC-093-admin-role.md)).

As received, asked who is notified of a new red flag and of a change the tool reverted on GitHub: "people can subscribe to issues and projects. Write and admin roles are autometically subscribed". That subscribers are the ones notified is the drafting agent's reading of this answer to "who is notified".
