# SRC-014: Gantt and Kanban show AI proposals as a pending overlay

While an AI proposal is under review, the Gantt and Kanban must show it as a pending overlay: a diff on both views in the browser, without touching the live plan.

Origin: the drafting agent's addition, as a way to show the stakeholder's "review it in the browser". No alternative was discussed.
