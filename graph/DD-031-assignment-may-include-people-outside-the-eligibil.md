# DD-031: Assignment may include people outside the eligibility expression

## Decision

An assignment may include people outside the eligibility expression. This is accepted.

## Alternatives considered

A hard reject (the drafting agent's suggestion), allowing it with a warning, or accepting it.

## Rationale

Some people can do anything and are not listed on every task. As received: "there are people who can do anything, they are not gonna be written in every task as 'any'".

## Trade-offs

Gives up rejecting such an assignment, or warning about it.

This decision serves [SRC-030](SRC-030-eligibility-is-an-and-or-expression-over-people-up.md), [SRC-032](SRC-032-assignment-is-a-concrete-list-of-people.md).
