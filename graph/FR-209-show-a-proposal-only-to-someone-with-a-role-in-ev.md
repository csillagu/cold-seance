# FR-209: Show a proposal only to someone with a role in every project it changes

The system must show a proposal, whole, to someone with any role in every project it changes, and refuse it as not visible to anyone without a role in one of them ([SRC-255](SRC-255-a-proposal-over-several-projects-is-seen-only-by.md), [FR-017](FR-017-tell-someone-explicitly-when-a-project-is-not-visi.md)).
