# FR-028: Store eligibility as an AND/OR expression up to two levels deep

The system must store, for each task, who can do it as a logical expression over people using AND and OR, up to two levels deep, such as `A AND (B OR C OR D)` or `(A AND B) OR C` ([SRC-029](SRC-029-two-separate-assignment-levels-who-can-and-who-wil.md), [SRC-030](SRC-030-eligibility-is-an-and-or-expression-over-people-up.md)).

It serves [UC-003](UC-003-assign-people-to-a-task.md).
