# SRC-157: A proposal spanning several projects is applied only when every part is approved

A proposal that spans several projects ([DD-025](DD-025-a-proposal-may-span-several-projects-each-project.md)) is applied only once every project's part of it has been approved.

As received, asked what happens when one project approves its part and another rejects its part or lets it expire: "both parts mist be approved for them to be applied".
