# SRC-254: A proposal over several projects is created only by a read-write member or admin of each

A proposal that changes several projects can be created only by someone with the read-write or admin role in each of the projects it changes.

As received, in the design session of 2026-11-15, on issue #130 (the proposal approval, SC-048), answering what SRC-245 left open. Asked "A proposal moves tasks in two projects, Apollo and Borealis. What role must its creator (or their AI token) hold to create it?", with the options "Write in each: read-write or admin in every project the proposal changes", "Write in at least one: read-write or admin in at least one of the projects, the other projects protected by their own approvals" and "Any role in each: read-write in at least one project and any role, read-only included, in the others", the owner chose: "Write in each".
