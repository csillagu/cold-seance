# FR-139: Record the approver as the author of an approved AI proposal's changes

When an approved AI proposal is applied, the system must record in the audit log the person who approved it as the one who made each change ([SRC-156](SRC-156-a-change-from-an-approved-ai-proposal-is-recorded.md), [SRC-095](SRC-095-every-change-is-recorded-in-an-audit-log.md)).

It serves [UC-012](UC-012-review-and-approve-an-ai-proposal.md).
