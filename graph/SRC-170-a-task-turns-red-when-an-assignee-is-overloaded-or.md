# SRC-170: A task turns red when an assignee is overloaded or on holiday during it

The people assigned to a task cannot do it in its time ([SRC-122](SRC-122-a-task-turns-red-when-its-assigned-people-cannot-d.md)) when one of them is overloaded or on holiday during the task.

As received, asked what, besides a day off inside the task, makes the assigned people unable to do it in its time: "assignee overload, holiday i cant think of anything else".
