# SRC-138: A cross-project view allows whatever the viewer can do from another view

In a view that covers several projects ([SRC-109](SRC-109-views-can-cover-several-projects.md)), a person can do with a card anything they could do with it from another view, where the view supports it. What they can do follows their role in the card's project, held directly ([DD-034](DD-034-roles-are-assigned-per-project.md)) or through a group ([SRC-139](SRC-139-project-access-also-comes-through-groups.md)).

As received, asked whether someone with the contributor role can mark their own cards in a cross-project view, when cards there were editable only where the viewer can write: "yes obviously, the point there is that anything can be done from that view that they can do from another view. (If the view supports)".
