# FR-191: Keep a milestone without duration, effort or AI share

The system must keep a milestone as taking no time, with no duration, effort or AI share ([SRC-236](SRC-236-a-milestone-is-a-kind-of-task-fixed-date-or-floati.md)), and reject any of them given for a milestone. A fixed-date milestone's only date is the date it is pinned to.

It serves [UC-027](UC-027-pin-a-milestone-to-a-fixed-date.md).
