# UC-026: Record an absence

## Goal

Make load reflect days a person is away.

The actor is not stated in the input: who records a person's absences is still open.

## Steps

1. The actor enters an absence for the person, such as vacation or sick leave, in whole days ([SRC-042](SRC-042-absences-set-a-person-s-capacity-to-0.md), [DD-011](DD-011-availability-is-tracked-in-whole-days.md)).

## Success criteria

- The person's capacity is 0 on the absent days, and the person load view shows any load on those days as overload ([SRC-042](SRC-042-absences-set-a-person-s-capacity-to-0.md), [SRC-011](SRC-011-person-load-view.md)).
