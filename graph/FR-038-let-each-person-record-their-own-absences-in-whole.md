# FR-038: Let each person record their own absences in whole days

The system must let each person record their own absences, such as vacation or sick leave ([SRC-129](SRC-129-each-person-records-their-own-absences.md), [SRC-042](SRC-042-absences-set-a-person-s-capacity-to-0.md)), in whole days ([DD-011](DD-011-availability-is-tracked-in-whole-days.md)).

It serves [UC-026](UC-026-record-my-absence.md).
