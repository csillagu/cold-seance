# SRC-239: A person cannot be assigned to a task twice

An assignment that names the same person more than once is rejected, rather than having the repeats dropped.

As received, in the design session of 2026-11-04, in the review of the eligibility and assignment module (SC-005, PR #76), asked whether rejecting duplicate assignees, rather than silently dropping them, is right: "yes reject".
