# SRC-255: A proposal over several projects is seen only by someone with a role in each

A proposal that changes several projects can be seen, and so a part of it approved, only by someone with a role in every project it changes; a read-only role is enough to see it. Every approver sees the whole proposal and its whole preview.

As received, in the design session of 2026-11-15, on issue #130 (the proposal approval, SC-048). Asked "Who may see a proposal that spans Apollo and Borealis, and so approve their own part?", with the options "Role in every project: every approver sees the whole proposal and its full preview" and "See only own parts: someone with a role in only some of the projects sees and approves just those parts, the changes to other projects hidden from them", the owner chose: "Role in every project".
