# FR-006: Make a parent task span its children's dates

The system must keep a parent task's dates spanning the dates of its subtasks, at every level of nesting ([SRC-020](SRC-020-subtask-parent-child-constraint.md)), and update them automatically within the project when a subtask's dates change ([SRC-003](SRC-003-constraints-reschedule-dates-automatically-within.md)).

It serves [UC-002](UC-002-reschedule-work-in-a-project.md) and [UC-021](UC-021-link-tasks-with-constraints.md).
