# FR-160: Keep at least one admin in every group

The system must not let a group lose its last admin: it must reject the last admin leaving the group ([SRC-145](SRC-145-a-group-s-last-admin-cannot-leave.md)).

It serves [UC-030](UC-030-manage-a-group-s-members.md) and [UC-031](UC-031-leave-a-group.md).
