# FR-190: Let a task be made a milestone, with a fixed date or floating

The system must let a task be made a milestone when it is created ([SRC-236](SRC-236-a-milestone-is-a-kind-of-task-fixed-date-or-floati.md)), and record whether the milestone is pinned to a fixed date, with that date ([SRC-022](SRC-022-fixed-date-milestones-constraint.md)), or floating.

It serves [UC-001](UC-001-plan-a-task-in-a-project.md) and [UC-027](UC-027-pin-a-milestone-to-a-fixed-date.md).
