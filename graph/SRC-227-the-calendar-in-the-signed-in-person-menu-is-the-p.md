# SRC-227: The calendar in the signed-in person menu is the personal calendar

Calendar, brought up by clicking the signed-in person ([SRC-221](SRC-221-clicking-the-signed-in-person-in-the-header-opens.md)), opens the person's own working calendar, not the company calendar.

As received, in the design session of 2026-11-03, asked whether calendar opens the personal working calendar, the company calendar, or both: "personal".
