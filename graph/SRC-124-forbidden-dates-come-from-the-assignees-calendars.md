# SRC-124: Forbidden dates come from the assignees' calendars

A task's forbidden dates come from the calendars of the people assigned to it ([SRC-120](SRC-120-each-person-maintains-their-own-working-calendar.md)). A day off for any one of them is a forbidden date for the task.

As received: "Forbidden dates should come from assignee calendars." Asked whether a date is forbidden when only some of several assigned people are off: "if a task needs 2 people, if one of them is of then obviously".
