# SRC-124: Forbidden dates come from the assignees' calendars

A task's forbidden dates come from the calendars of the people assigned to it ([SRC-120](SRC-120-each-person-maintains-their-own-working-calendar.md)).

As received: "Forbidden dates should come from assignee calendars."

Not stated: whether a date is forbidden when only some of several assigned people are off.
