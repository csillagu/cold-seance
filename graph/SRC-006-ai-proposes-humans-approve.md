# SRC-006: AI proposes, humans approve

AI changes to the plan arrive as proposals that a person reviews and edits in the browser before they are applied. AI agents change the plan only through proposals that a person approves.

As received: "AI proposes, and human can edit AND review it in the browser itself, only then does the plan gets overwritten".
