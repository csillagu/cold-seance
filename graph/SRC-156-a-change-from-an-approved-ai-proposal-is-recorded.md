# SRC-156: A change from an approved AI proposal is recorded as made by its approver

When an approved AI proposal is applied ([SRC-081](SRC-081-on-approval-all-changes-are-applied-together.md)), the audit log ([SRC-095](SRC-095-every-change-is-recorded-in-an-audit-log.md)) records the person who approved it as the one who made the change.

As received, asked who is recorded as making a change that an approved AI proposal applies: "Approver of course". Which channel is recorded for it was not answered.
