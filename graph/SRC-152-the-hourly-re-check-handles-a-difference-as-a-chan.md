# SRC-152: The hourly re-check handles a difference as a change that has just arrived

When the hourly re-check of linked issues ([SRC-073](SRC-073-hourly-reconciliation-of-all-linked-issues.md)) finds a difference, the tool handles it exactly as if the change had just arrived from GitHub.

As received, asked whether a difference the re-check finds is handled exactly as if the change had just arrived: "yes".
