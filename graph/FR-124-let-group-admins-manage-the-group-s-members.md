# FR-124: Let group admins manage the group's members

The system must let any member holding the admin role in a group add people to it with a role, change their roles and remove them ([SRC-144](SRC-144-the-group-admin-manages-the-group-s-members.md), [SRC-139](SRC-139-project-access-also-comes-through-groups.md)).

It serves [UC-018](UC-018-create-a-group-and-give-it-access-to-projects.md) and [UC-030](UC-030-manage-a-group-s-members.md).
