# SRC-237: An eligibility expression deeper than two levels is rejected

An eligibility expression nested deeper than two levels is rejected, not stored.

As received, in the design session of 2026-11-04, in the review of the eligibility and assignment module (SC-005, PR #76), asked whether rejecting an eligibility expression three levels deep is right: "yes".
