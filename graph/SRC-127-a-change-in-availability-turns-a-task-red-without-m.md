# SRC-127: A change in availability turns a task red without moving it

A change made after a task is planned that leaves the assigned people unable to do the task in its time, such as someone adding a day off or someone being assigned, does not move the task; it turns red ([SRC-122](SRC-122-a-task-turns-red-when-its-assigned-people-cannot-d.md)).

As received: the drafting agent asked whether "… changes made afterwards turn the task red instead of moving it. Examples are someone adding a day off, or someone being assigned." The stakeholder answered: "correct". The part of the question left out here, that your own moves are blocked, is recorded in SRC-123.
