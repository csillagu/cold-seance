# FR-085: Notify reviewers of a proposal waiting for review

When a proposal is waiting for review, the system must notify the people who can review it: those with the read-write role in a project it changes ([SRC-015](SRC-015-notifications-for-new-red-flags-proposals-waiting.md), [SRC-080](SRC-080-a-person-with-write-access-edits-approves-or-rejec.md), [DD-025](DD-025-a-proposal-may-span-several-projects-each-project.md)).

It serves [UC-011](UC-011-an-ai-agent-proposes-a-plan-change.md) and [UC-012](UC-012-review-and-approve-an-ai-proposal.md).
