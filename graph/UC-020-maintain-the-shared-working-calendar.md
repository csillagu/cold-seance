# UC-020: Maintain the shared working calendar

## Goal

Keep working days and public holidays right, so that scheduling skips the days nobody works.

The actor is not stated in the input: who maintains the shared calendar is still open.

## Steps

1. The actor sets which days of the week are working days ([SRC-044](SRC-044-a-shared-working-calendar-defines-working-days-and.md)).
2. They enter the public holidays ([SRC-044](SRC-044-a-shared-working-calendar-defines-working-days-and.md)).

## Success criteria

- Scheduling skips non-working days, and durations count only working days ([SRC-044](SRC-044-a-shared-working-calendar-defines-working-days-and.md), [DD-006](DD-006-durations-are-whole-working-days.md)).
