# SRC-218: The receiving project's admin gives up the receiving role

The admin of a repository's receiving project ([SRC-178](SRC-178-the-receiving-project-is-marked-by-a-project-admin.md)) is the one who gives up its receiving role ([SRC-216](SRC-216-a-second-receiving-project-is-refused-until-the-f.md)).

As received, in the design session of 2026-11-03, asked who can give up the receiving role, with Claude's reading that it is the receiving project's own admin: "the receiving project's admin, yes".
