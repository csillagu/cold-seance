# SRC-247: A proposal can change anything in a project's plan

A proposal can make any change to a project's plan, that is to its tasks; not to its members, roles, calendars, repositories or settings.

As received, in the design session of 2026-11-06, in the work on the proposal store (SC-010, issue #114). Claude reported that DD-020 lists the planning inputs a proposal may change (new tasks, durations, effort, AI share, constraints, fixed dates, eligibility and assignments) but not whether it may also rename a task, change its description, cancel it or change its status. The owner answered: "Proposal can do anyhting." Asked which changes that covers, the owner chose "Anything in the plan": any change to a project's tasks, not to its members, roles, calendars, repository mapping or settings.
