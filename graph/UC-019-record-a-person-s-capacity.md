# UC-019: Record a person's capacity

## Goal

Measure a person's load against how much they actually work.

The actor is not stated in the input: who records a person's capacity is still open.

## Steps

1. The actor sets the person's capacity, for example 100% full-time or 80% part-time ([SRC-041](SRC-041-each-person-has-a-capacity.md)).

## Success criteria

- The person load view measures the person's load against the new capacity ([SRC-041](SRC-041-each-person-has-a-capacity.md), [SRC-011](SRC-011-person-load-view.md)).
