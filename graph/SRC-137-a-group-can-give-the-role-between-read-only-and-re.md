# SRC-137: A group can give the role between read-only and read-write

A group can give its members the role between read-only and read-write ([SRC-136](SRC-136-a-role-between-read-only-and-read-write-lets-a-per.md)), just as it can give the other project roles.

As received, asked whether a group can give this role too: "yes".
