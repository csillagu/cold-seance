# FR-199: Mark the people on a task who cannot read its project

The system must mark, wherever a task's eligibility expression or assignment is shown in the UI, each person in it who cannot read the task's project ([SRC-238](SRC-238-people-who-lose-read-access-stay-on-a-task-marked.md)).

It serves [UC-003](UC-003-assign-people-to-a-task.md).
