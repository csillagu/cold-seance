# FR-010: Treat company holidays and weekends as forbidden dates for every task

The system must treat every company holiday and every weekend day as a forbidden date for every task, whoever is assigned to it ([SRC-126](SRC-126-company-holidays-are-forbidden-for-every-task.md)). The weekend days are those set in the global calendar ([SRC-161](SRC-161-the-weekend-is-set-in-the-global-calendar.md)); a person's own calendar cannot change them ([SRC-162](SRC-162-a-person-s-own-calendar-has-holidays-not-its-own-w.md)).

It serves [UC-002](UC-002-reschedule-work-in-a-project.md) and [UC-028](UC-028-maintain-the-company-holiday-calendar.md).
