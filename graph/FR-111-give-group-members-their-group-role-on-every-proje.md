# FR-111: Give group members their group role on every project in the group

The system must give each member of a group their group role (read, contributor, write or admin) on every project in the group ([SRC-139](SRC-139-project-access-also-comes-through-groups.md), [SRC-137](SRC-137-a-group-can-give-the-contributor-role.md)), for as long as they are a member and the project is in the group.

It serves [UC-017](UC-017-create-a-project.md), [UC-018](UC-018-create-a-group-and-give-it-access-to-projects.md), [UC-030](UC-030-manage-a-group-s-members.md), [UC-031](UC-031-leave-a-group.md) and [UC-032](UC-032-delete-a-group.md).
