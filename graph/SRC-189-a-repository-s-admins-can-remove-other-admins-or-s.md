# SRC-189: A repository's admins can remove other admins or step down

A repository's admins ([SRC-180](SRC-180-repositories-are-their-own-item-in-the-tool-with-a.md)) can remove other admins of the repository, or step down themselves, as long as the repository keeps at least one admin ([SRC-188](SRC-188-a-repository-cannot-be-left-without-an-admin.md)).

As received, asked whether repository admins can remove other admins, or step down themselves, as long as one admin remains: "yes".
