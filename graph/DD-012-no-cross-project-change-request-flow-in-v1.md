# DD-012: No cross-project change-request flow in v1

## Decision

There is no separate cross-project change-request flow in v1. Owners get an in-app notification only.

## Alternatives considered

A "request a change" flow, versus in-app notification only.

## Trade-offs

Gives up a change-request flow in v1.
