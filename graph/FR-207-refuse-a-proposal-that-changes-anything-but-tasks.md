# FR-207: Refuse a proposal that changes anything but tasks

The system must refuse a proposal that changes anything other than a project's tasks, such as its members, roles, calendars, repositories or settings, and store nothing of it ([SRC-247](SRC-247-a-proposal-can-change-anything-in-a-project-s-pla.md)).

It serves [UC-011](UC-011-an-ai-agent-proposes-a-plan-change.md).
