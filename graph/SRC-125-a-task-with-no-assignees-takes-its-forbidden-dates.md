# SRC-125: Without assignee calendars, forbidden dates come from the global calendar

When a task has no assigned people, or an assigned person has not filled in their own calendar, the forbidden dates come from the global calendar instead.

As received, following "Forbidden dates should come from assignee calendars.": "If none, lets have a global one that has like company holidays". Asked whether "if none" meant a task with no assigned people or an assigned person without a calendar: "Both".
