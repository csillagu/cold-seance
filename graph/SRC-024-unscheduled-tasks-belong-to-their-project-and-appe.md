# SRC-024: Unscheduled tasks belong to their project and appear in every view kind

Tasks without planned dates are "unscheduled". An unscheduled task belongs to its project like any other task; there is no separate inbox.

As received: "these should go into the project, but without scheduling. There will be tasks anyway that has no scheduling, it can be put into the kanban, in the gant, it will just lay at the bottom of the screen. So in general, all items are shown on all view KINDS."
