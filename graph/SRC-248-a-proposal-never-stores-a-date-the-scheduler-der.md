# SRC-248: A proposal never stores a date the scheduler derives

A proposal never stores a date the scheduler derives, even though it can change anything in a project's plan.

As received, in the design session of 2026-11-06, in the work on the proposal store (SC-010, issue #114). Right after answering that a proposal can change anything in the plan, the owner was asked whether DD-020's rule, that a proposal never stores dates the scheduler derives and its preview is always recomputed against the live plan, still holds. The owner chose "Yes, still holds".
