# DD-003: Notifications are in-app only

## Decision

Notifications are in-app only. There are no email, Slack or other external notifications.

## Alternatives considered

Email, Slack, or in-app notifications.

## Trade-offs

Gives up email and Slack notifications.

This decision serves [SRC-015](SRC-015-notifications-for-new-red-flags-proposals-waiting.md).
