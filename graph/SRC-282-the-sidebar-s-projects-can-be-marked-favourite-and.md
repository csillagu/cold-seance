# SRC-282: The sidebar's projects can be marked favourite and searched within

A person can mark projects as favourites, and their favourites are listed first in the navigation's list of projects. Marking is per account and kept on the server, so it follows the person to any browser. The navigation also gets a search box that searches only within the projects it lists.

As received, in the design session of 2026-11-23, recorded in issue #256 (the UI/UX review of the demo deployment). Asked, about item 4 ("The sidebar has nothing to narrow it down, such as favourites, recent projects or grouping"), "What should narrow it down?", with the options "Favourites" ("A person stars projects; starred ones are listed first. Stored per account on the server, so they follow the person across browsers."), "Recent projects", "Filter box" ("A small text box above the list that narrows it as you type.") and "Nothing more", the owner chose "Favourites" and answered, in their own words: "Serach box that only searches within  the projects."

Not stated: how the search box differs from the header's project search ([SRC-220](SRC-220-the-header-holds-a-project-search-bar.md)) beyond searching only within the listed projects; whether favourites are also shown anywhere but the navigation.
