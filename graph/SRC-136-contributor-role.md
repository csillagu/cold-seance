# SRC-136: Contributor role

The contributor role is a project role above read-only ([SRC-091](SRC-091-read-only-role.md)) and below read-write ([SRC-092](SRC-092-read-write-role.md)). A contributor can mark a task they are assigned to ([SRC-032](SRC-032-assignment-is-a-concrete-list-of-people.md)) as in progress, on hold or finished.

As received: "User with a specific role (lower than write, higher than read) marks his assigned task in progress/on hold/ finished."

A contributor cannot edit a task's other fields, cancel a task, or reopen a finished one.

That the role covers only tasks the person is assigned to was the drafting agent's reading of "his assigned task"; asked to confirm, the stakeholder said: "yes". Asked whether the role can also edit other fields of its own tasks, cancel them or reopen a finished one, the stakeholder said: "none of these". Asked for a name, the stakeholder asked the drafting agent to propose one; it proposed "Contributor", and the stakeholder said: "contributor is fine".
