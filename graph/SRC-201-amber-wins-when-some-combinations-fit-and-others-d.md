# SRC-201: Amber wins when some combinations fit and others don't

For a task with eligible but no assigned people, the red, amber and green rating of [SRC-035](SRC-035-red-amber-and-green-load-for-eligible-but-unassign.md) gives both amber and green when some valid combinations of people overload someone and at least one fits. In that case the rating is amber.

As received, asked which colour applies when amber ("some valid combinations overload someone and others don't") and green ("at least one valid combination fits") both hold: "Amber wins".
