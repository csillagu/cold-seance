# SRC-121: Scheduling skips non-working days

Scheduling skips non-working days.

Origin: carried over from the retired SRC-044, where it was the drafting agent's addition under "capacity and calendars" and was never discussed. Since [SRC-120](SRC-120-each-person-maintains-their-own-working-calendar.md) there is no shared calendar, so non-working days come from each person's own calendar. Whose calendar applies to a task with several assigned people, or with none, is not yet stated.
