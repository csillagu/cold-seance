# FR-009: Take forbidden dates from the global calendar for a task with no assigned people

When a task has no assigned people, the system must take its forbidden dates from the global calendar ([SRC-125](SRC-125-a-task-with-no-assignees-takes-its-forbidden-dates.md)), which holds company holidays and weekends ([SRC-131](SRC-131-the-global-calendar-holds-company-holidays-and-week.md)).

It serves [UC-002](UC-002-reschedule-work-in-a-project.md).
