# FR-013: Show a red violation when a fixed-date milestone is pushed

When work a milestone depends on would push it past its fixed date, the system must leave the milestone on its date and mark the violation in red ([SRC-022](SRC-022-fixed-date-milestones-constraint.md)), on the Gantt ([SRC-010](SRC-010-gantt-view.md)).

It serves [UC-002](UC-002-reschedule-work-in-a-project.md), [UC-021](UC-021-link-tasks-with-constraints.md) and [UC-027](UC-027-pin-a-milestone-to-a-fixed-date.md).
