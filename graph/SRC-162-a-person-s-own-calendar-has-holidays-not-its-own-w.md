# SRC-162: A person's own calendar has holidays, not its own weekend

A person cannot set a weekend of their own in their own calendar ([SRC-120](SRC-120-each-person-maintains-their-own-working-calendar.md)); they can enter as many holidays as they want.

As received, asked whether a person's own calendar can make a weekend day a working day for them: "no, person can have holidays, as much as they want".
