# FR-010: Treat company holidays as forbidden dates for every task

The system must treat every company holiday as a forbidden date for every task, whoever is assigned to it, as it treats a weekend ([SRC-126](SRC-126-company-holidays-are-forbidden-for-every-task.md)).

Not stated: which days of the week are the weekend, and whether a person's own calendar can make a weekend day a working day for them.

It serves [UC-002](UC-002-reschedule-work-in-a-project.md) and [UC-028](UC-028-maintain-the-company-holiday-calendar.md).
