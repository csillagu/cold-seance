# DD-041: The tool is built rather than adopted from Plane or OpenProject

## Decision

The tool is built as its own project instead of adopting Plane or OpenProject.

## Alternatives considered

Plane, OpenProject, or building the tool. The drafting agent's record says this choice "was clearly weighed" but that no item recorded it.

## Rationale

Not stated.

## Trade-offs

Gives up adopting Plane or OpenProject.

## Serves

This decision serves [SRC-001](SRC-001-the-tool-is-self-hosted-and-open-source.md).

Origin: the alternatives come from the drafting agent's record. Asked in the design session of 2026-11-02 whether they chose to build their own tool, and why not Plane or OpenProject, the stakeholder answered: "Build". The reason was not given.
