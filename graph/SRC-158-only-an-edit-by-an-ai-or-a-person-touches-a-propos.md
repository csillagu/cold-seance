# SRC-158: Only an edit, by an AI or a person, touches a proposal

For the 24-hour expiry ([DD-024](DD-024-proposals-untouched-for-24-hours-expire.md)), a proposal is touched when it is edited, by an AI or by a person.

As received, asked what counts as touching a proposal: "edit. Ai or human".
