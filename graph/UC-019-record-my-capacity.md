# UC-019: Record my capacity

## Goal

Have one's load measured against how much one actually works.

## Steps

1. The actor sets their own capacity, for example 100% full-time or 80% part-time ([SRC-130](SRC-130-each-person-records-their-own-capacity.md), [SRC-041](SRC-041-each-person-has-a-capacity.md)).

## Success criteria

- The person load view measures the actor's load against the new capacity ([SRC-041](SRC-041-each-person-has-a-capacity.md), [SRC-011](SRC-011-person-load-view.md)).
