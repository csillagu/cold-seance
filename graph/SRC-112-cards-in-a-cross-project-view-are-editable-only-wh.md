# SRC-112: Cards in a cross-project view are editable only where the viewer can write

In a view that covers several projects ([SRC-109](SRC-109-views-can-cover-several-projects.md)), a card is editable only if the viewer can write to its project, through a direct role ([DD-034](DD-034-roles-are-assigned-per-project.md)) or a group ([DD-028](DD-028-project-access-also-comes-through-groups.md)). The other cards are read-only.

Proposed in the design session and accepted: "perfect".
