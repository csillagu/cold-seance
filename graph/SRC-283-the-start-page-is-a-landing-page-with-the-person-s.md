# SRC-283: The start page is a landing page with the person's open tasks, proposals to review and latest notifications

The start page is a well-made landing page for the signed-in person. It shows their open tasks (the tasks assigned to them that are not done or cancelled, across their projects), the AI proposals waiting for their review in projects where they can approve them, and their latest notifications, linking to the full list.

As received, in the design session of 2026-11-23, recorded in issue #256 (the UI/UX review of the demo deployment). Asked, about item 8 ("The start page holds only \"Open a project from the navigation.\""), "What should it show the signed-in person?", with the options "My open tasks" ("Tasks assigned to me that are not done or cancelled, across my projects."), "Proposals to review" ("AI proposals waiting for review in projects where I can approve them."), "Red flags" and "Latest notifications" ("My few most recent notifications, linking to the full list."), the owner chose "My open tasks", "Proposals to review" and "Latest notifications", not "Red flags", and added: "Create a nice landing page that includes all these".

Not stated: how many tasks and notifications are shown, and in what order.
